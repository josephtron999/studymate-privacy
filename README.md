# studymate-privacy

**Last updated:** January 2026  
**Owner:** Orrin Hager (Individual Developer)

## **1. Introduction**
StudyMate (“the App”) is an educational tool designed to help users study more efficiently using AI‑powered text extraction and problem analysis. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

By using StudyMate, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## **2. Information We Collect**
StudyMate collects only the minimum information necessary to operate the App’s core features.

### **2.1 Photos and Screenshots Uploaded by the User**
- When you upload a photo or screenshot for study assistance, the image is processed temporarily to extract text and generate results.
- Images are **not stored**, saved, or retained after processing.

### **2.2 Camera Access**
- The App may request access to your device’s camera so you can capture images for study purposes.
- Camera access is used **only** to take photos within the App and is never used in the background.

### **2.3 AI Processing (OCR & Text Extraction)**
- Uploaded images are sent to AI models solely for:
  - Optical Character Recognition (OCR)
  - Text extraction
  - Study assistance and explanation generation
- Images and extracted text are **not stored** by StudyMate.

## **3. No Data Storage**
StudyMate does **not** store:
- Photos or screenshots  
- Extracted text  
- Personal information  
- Usage history  

All processing is temporary and used only to generate results for the user.

## **4. Third‑Party Services**
StudyMate uses the following third‑party service:

### **RevenueCat**
Used for:
- Managing in‑app purchases  
- Subscription validation  
- Entitlement management  

RevenueCat may collect:
- Anonymous device identifiers  
- Purchase history  
- Subscription status  

RevenueCat does **not** receive your photos, screenshots, or study content.

You can review RevenueCat’s privacy policy on their official website.

## **5. No User Accounts**
StudyMate does **not** require or support user accounts.  
We do not collect:
- Names  
- Emails  
- Passwords  
- Profile information  

## **6. Children’s Privacy**
StudyMate does **not** target minors.  
The App is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## **7. How We Use Information**
Any information processed by StudyMate is used solely to:
- Extract text from images  
- Provide study explanations  
- Improve the user experience  

We do **not**:
- Sell data  
- Share data with advertisers  
- Track users across apps or websites  

## **8. Data Security**
Although StudyMate does not store user data, we take reasonable measures to ensure that temporary processing is handled securely and responsibly.

## **9. Your Rights**
Because StudyMate does not store personal data, there is no stored information to access, modify, or delete.
